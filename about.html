Who we are

About Apex

Led by directors with more than 50 years’ combined experience

APEX Plumbing & Civil specialises in delivering high-quality plumbing, civil and infrastructure solutions across a diverse range of projects, including new residential homes, multi-residential developments, townhouse complexes, commercial buildings, industrial facilities and land subdivision projects.

With a strong focus on safety, quality and project delivery, we work collaboratively with developers, builders and consultants to deliver cost-effective, reliable infrastructure solutions that meet programme requirements and exceed client expectations.
